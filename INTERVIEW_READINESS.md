# Interview Readiness Guide – AI Infrastructure Principal Architect

## Purpose
Equip you to demonstrate enterprise-wide architectural leadership, contract negotiation, and compliance stewardship during interviews.

## Core Competencies to Highlight
- Multi-cloud strategy, contractual negotiation, and vendor management  
- Regulatory compliance, risk registers, and audit-ready documentation  
- Executive influence, board-level communication, and financial modelling  
- Mentoring architects and aligning global teams

## Preparation Checklist
1. Gather artefacts: contract checklists, cost models, regulatory matrices.  
2. Frame each story around business impact (savings, risk reduction, growth).  
3. Prepare executive-friendly visuals (roadmaps, portfolios, escalation maps).  
4. Practice explaining trade-offs in procurement, compliance, and technical design.

## Mock Interview Structure (60 minutes)
| Segment | Objective | Repository Pointers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 min | Strategic introduction | Root README |
| 20 min | Multi-cloud or compliance deep dive | `projects/project-302` / `project-305` |
| 15 min | Executive communication scenario | Governance and stakeholder modules |
| 10 min | Mentorship / succession planning | Leadership notes |
| 5 min | Candidate questions | See below |

## Sample Technical Prompts
- “Explain how you structured multi-cloud contracts to manage risk and cost.”  
- “Walk through your approach to meeting data residency and regulatory requirements.”  
- “How do you prioritise architecture investments across global teams?”

## Behavioral Prompts
- Navigating conflicting executive agendas  
- Handling high-stakes escalations or audit findings  
- Coaching regional architects or principal engineers

## Questions to Ask the Interviewer
- “How does your organisation fund and review large-scale AI infrastructure programmes?”  
- “What governance structures are in place for architecture decisions?”  
- “What executive stakeholders will I partner with most closely?”

## Next Steps
Curate a personal interview packet with contracts, decision logs, and executive briefs. Update after each major negotiation or compliance milestone.***
